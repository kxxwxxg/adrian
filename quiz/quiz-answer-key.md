## Part A: Vocabulary (5 marks)

1. **hat**
   - h **a** t
   - *Hint: You wear this on your head to keep the sun away.*

2. **dress**
   - d r **e** **s** s
   - *Hint: Girls often wear this. It is one piece.*

3. **socks**
   - s **o** c k s
   - *Hint: You wear these on your feet before you put on shoes.*

4. **jeans**
   - j e **a** n s
   - *Hint: These are long pants. They are often blue.*

5. **T-shirt**
   - T - s **h** i r t

## Part B: Grammar - His or Her? (5 marks)

1. **Her**
   - This is Yoyo. ( *Her* ) dress is red.
   - *Rule: Use "her" for girls/females.*

2. **His**
   - This is Tim. ( *His* ) cap is green.
   - *Rule: Use "his" for boys/males.*

3. **His**
   - Look at the boy. ( *His* ) shoes are brown.
   - *Rule: Use "his" for boys/males.*

4. **Her**
   - Look at the girl. ( *Her* ) skirt is pink.
   - *Rule: Use "her" for girls/females.*

5. **His**
   - This is Mr. Lee. ( *His* ) shirt is white.
   - *Rule: Use "his" for men/males.*

---

## Part C: Grammar - Is or Are? (5 marks)

1. **is**
   - The hat **is** purple.
   - *Rule: Use "is" for singular items.*

2. **are** ⚠️ *Common Mistake Alert!*
   - My trousers **are** green.
   - *Rule: Trousers have two legs, so use "are" (plural).*
   - *Students often write "is" - this is a typical P1 mistake!*

3. **are**
   - Her socks **are** yellow.
   - *Rule: Socks come in pairs (two items), so use "are".*

4. **is**
   - The blouse **is** pink.
   - *Rule: Use "is" for singular items.*

5. **are**
   - His shorts **are** blue.
   - *Rule: Shorts have two legs, so use "are" (plural).*

## Part D: Reading Comprehension (5 marks)

### True or False:

1. **F** (False)
   - Kelly dresses up as a **princess**, not a pirate.

2. **T** (True)
   - Kelly's dress **is** pink and purple. (Correct as written in text)

3. **F** (False)
   - Billy's T-shirt is **black and white**, not blue.

4. **T** (True)
   - Billy's trousers **are** brown. (Correct as written in text)

### Written Answer:

5. **White** (or "They are white")
   - What colour are Kelly's shoes?
   - *Answer found in text: "Her shoes are white."*

## 💡 Teaching Tips & Common Mistakes

### 1. The "Trousers/Jeans/Shorts" Rule
- **Problem:** In Part C, Question 2, students often write: *"My trousers is green."*
- **Why this happens:** Students see one item and think it's singular.
- **Teaching approach:** Emphasize that trousers, jeans, and shorts have **two legs**, making them plural.
- **Visual aid:** Show students that trousers = 2 legs → use "are"

### 2. Possessive Adjectives
- **1 Boy = His** (Tim, Mr. Lee, Billy)
- **1 Girl = Her** (Yoyo, Kelly)
- **2+ People = Their** (not tested but useful extension)

### 3. Subject-Verb Agreement
- **Singular items (hat, dress, blouse) = is**
- **Plural items (trousers, jeans, shorts, socks) = are**
- **Mnemonic:** "Two legs = plural = are"

### 4. Reading Comprehension
- Encourage students to underline key information
- For True/False questions, ask them to find the evidence in the text
- For written answers, accept both "White" and "They are white"

## Extension Activities

### For Advanced Students:
1. **Writing Practice:**
   - Write 3 sentences about your own clothes using "is" or "are"
   - Example: "My shirt is red. My shoes are blue."

2. **Drawing:**
   - Draw yourself and describe your outfit using possessive adjectives
   - Example: "This is me. My dress is pink. My shoes are yellow."

3. **Speaking:**
   - In pairs, describe a friend's clothes
   - Example: "This is John. His shirt is green. His shorts are blue."

## Notes for Teachers/Parents

### When to Use This Quiz:
- End-of-unit assessment
- Practice homework
- Classroom activity (15-20 minutes)
- Parent-child learning time

### How to Administer:
1. Explain that images show the clothing items
2. Read questions aloud if students have reading difficulties
3. Allow 20-25 minutes for completion
4. Use answer key for quick marking
5. Discuss common mistakes, especially the "trousers are" rule

### Feedback Tips:
- Praise correct use of "are" with plural items (this is tricky!)
- Point out where possessive adjectives were used correctly
- Celebrate reading comprehension success
- Review incorrect answers positively as learning opportunities
