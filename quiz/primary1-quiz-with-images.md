### Part A: Vocabulary (5 marks)
**Fill in the missing letters.**

1. **h** \_\_ **t**

2. **d** **r** \_\_ \_\_ **s**

3. **s** \_\_ **c** **k** **s**

4. **j** **e** \_\_ **n** **s**

5. **T** - **s** \_\_ **i** **r** **t**

---

### Part B: Grammar - His or Her? (5 marks)
**Circle correct answer.**

1. This is Yoyo. ( His / Her ) dress is red.

2. This is Tim. ( His / Her ) cap is green.

3. Look at the boy. ( His / Her ) shoes are brown.

4. Look at the girl. ( His / Her ) skirt is pink.

5. This is Mr. Lee. ( His / Her ) shirt is white.

---

### Part C: Grammar - Is or Are? (5 marks)
**Fill in the blanks with "is" or "are".**
*(Remember: Use "are" for things with two legs like trousers, shorts, and jeans!)*

1. The hat \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ purple.

2. My trousers \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ green.

3. Her socks \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ yellow.

4. The blouse \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ pink.

5. His shorts \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ blue.

---

### Part D: Reading Comprehension (5 marks)
**Read the short text and answer the questions.**

> Today is Book Day. The children dress up as their favourite characters.
>
> **Kelly** is happy. She dresses up as a princess. Her dress is pink and purple. Her shoes are white.
>
> **Billy** dresses up as a pirate. His T-shirt is black and white. His trousers are brown. He looks smart!

**Write T (True) or F (False).**

1. Kelly dresses up as a pirate. (\_\_\_\_\_)

2. Kelly's dress is pink and purple. (\_\_\_\_\_)

3. Billy's T-shirt is blue. (\_\_\_\_\_)

4. Billy's trousers are brown. (\_\_\_\_\_)

**Answer the question:**

5. What colour are Kelly's shoes?
