## 💡 Teaching Tips

### Before the Quiz:
1. **Review vocabulary:**
   - Hat, dress, socks, jeans, T-shirt
   - Colors: red, blue, green, yellow, purple, pink, brown, black, white

2. **Practice grammar:**
   - His vs. Her (possessive adjectives)
   - Is vs. Are (subject-verb agreement)
   - **Special focus:** Trousers, jeans, shorts = are (plural!)

3. **Reading practice:**
   - Read the Book Day story aloud
   - Discuss Kelly (princess) and Billy (pirate)
   - Point out key information

### During the Quiz:
1. **Provide support:**
   - Read questions aloud if needed
   - Explain difficult words
   - Encourage students to try their best

2. **Time management:**
   - Set a timer (15-20 minutes)
   - Remind students of remaining time
   - Allow extra time if needed

3. **Positive reinforcement:**
   - Praise effort and progress
   - Correct mistakes gently
   - Celebrate achievements

### After the Quiz:
1. **Review answers:**
   - Go through quiz together
   - Explain why answers are correct
   - Discuss common mistakes

2. **Focus on learning:**
   - "Trousers are green" (not "is")
   - Possessive adjectives: His (boys) vs. Her (girls)
   - Subject-verb agreement rules

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this quiz, students should be able to:

### Vocabulary:
✅ Identify and name 5 clothing items (hat, dress, socks, jeans, T-shirt)
✅ Spell basic clothing vocabulary correctly
✅ Recognize clothing from images

### Grammar:
✅ Use possessive adjectives correctly (His/Her)
✅ Understand singular vs. plural (is/are)
✅ Apply "two legs = plural = are" rule for trousers/jeans/shorts

### Reading Comprehension:
✅ Read and understand short text about clothing
✅ Identify True/False statements
✅ Extract specific information from text
✅ Answer written questions about text

## 📊 Assessment Guidelines

### Grading Scale:
- **18-20 marks** (90-100%) - Excellent ⭐⭐⭐
- **15-17 marks** (75-89%) - Very Good ⭐⭐
- **12-14 marks** (60-74%) - Good ⭐
- **10-11 marks** (50-59%) - Satisfactory
- **Below 10 marks** (<50%) - Needs Improvement

### What to Look For:
1. **Correct spelling** in Part A
2. **Proper possessive use** in Part B
3. **Subject-verb agreement** in Part C (especially "are" with trousers)
4. **Reading comprehension accuracy** in Part D

### Common Mistakes to Address:
- "My trousers **is** green" → Should be "are"
- Confusing "His" and "Her"
- Spelling errors in vocabulary
- Misunderstanding reading comprehension questions

## 🎨 Extension Activities

### For Advanced Students:
1. **Creative Writing:**
   - Write a story about Kelly and Billy
   - Use vocabulary and grammar from quiz

2. **Drawing & Labeling:**
   - Draw your own clothes
   - Label each item with correct possessive adjective
   - Example: "This is me. My shirt is blue."

3. **Speaking Practice:**
   - Describe what classmates are wearing
   - Use "is/are" and "his/her" correctly

### For Homework:
1. **Vocabulary Practice:**
   - Write each word 3 times
   - Draw a picture for each word
   - Make flashcards

2. **Grammar Drills:**
   - Complete 10 sentences using "is" or "are"
   - Label 10 items with "his" or "her"

3. **Reading Extension:**
   - Read story again and answer new questions
   - Rewrite story with different characters
