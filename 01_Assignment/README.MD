# Show and Tell (Christmas Assignment)

## Show and Tell
**Topic:** My favourite Animal

Show a picture of your favourite animal and tell your teacher:

1. What is your favourite animal?
2. How does it look?
3. Why is it your favourite animal?
4. Are there any interesting facts about the animal?

The recording should be at least 30 seconds long.

## Tips for the Assignment

### Choosing a suitable animal image
This assignment is about “My favourite animal,” so a clear, cute, front-facing animal photo works best.

Pick a picture where:
- The animal’s whole body or face is easy to see.
- The background is simple, so your child can point at the animal.
- The animal looks friendly, not scary (good for primary level).

**Good options:**
- A cool **snake** slithering in the grass.
- A colorful animal like a parrot, clownfish, or butterfly.
- A popular wild animal like a panda, lion (not too fierce), or giraffe.

### How to pick and use the picture
- Search in Google Images or Bing:
  - “snake cartoon for kids”
  - “panda cartoon picture”
  - “colorful parrot photo for children”
- Save one picture and print it, or show it on a tablet during the recording.

### Example script (if you choose a snake)
- “My favourite animal is the snake.”
- “It is long, has scales, and slithers on the ground.”
- “I like snakes because they come in many colors and patterns.”
- “An interesting fact is that snakes smell with their tongues!”
- “Thank you for listening to my show and tell about my favourite animal!”
